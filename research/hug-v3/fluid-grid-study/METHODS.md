# Methods and qualifications

The controlling source is the archived threshold-0.015, breathing-on Hug v3 experiment in a periodic unit cube, not a circular basin. The source model is a synthetic coupled material/social candidate, not calibrated physical evidence. Prime ticks 37 and 41 label times 1.1 and 1.2. The archived 10% Q neighborhood supplies the observer bundle; Q does not truncate the vorticity line and is distinct from threshold 0.015.

The earlier Lean checks establish the elementary nested-window rule and exact comparisons of the archived recorded decimals. They do not verify these integrations, their convergence, or their physical interpretation. This package adds numerical checks only; it does not modify the Lean publication.

The unchanged fluid/material equations were rerun from time zero. The baseline was approved against the original sealed declarations. Refinements use an explicit runner-side numerical declaration changing only the Eulerian grid count; the actual Heun time step is supplied independently and stays below the original declared cap. Physical forcing remains the same periodic acceleration with peak component 0.5 and period 0.6. Event starts and writes occur at the same physical times and in the same before/after order. Each run recomputes and quantizes its event payload from its own evolving state. Event CRC/payload changes are reported, not replaced by archived payloads.

At every spatial resolution the initial heat is the same archived 16³ Fourier polynomial, rather than a newly sampled analytic heat profile. Initial velocity/context are zero and other initial arrays match. Omitted initial Fourier coefficients outside the retained ±5 band are only numerical roundoff. Mechanical/thermal kernel widths and the 384 material quadrature points remain fixed. This study cannot establish material-quadrature or full continuum convergence. Finer-than-32³ grids, dt below 0.00025, longer times, alternative model laws and physical calibration remain untested.

The 801 original marker IDs persist. Extra fixed A/B and center labels are obtained by inverting all 550 original Heun maps from prime 37 to time zero, then checked by full forward transport (maximum component roundtrip error 1.60e-16). Fixed pairs preserve particle identity but can have a nonzero initial gap at prime 37 in a refined run. Reselected pairs are built at ±0.09375 arclength along each run’s prime-37 vorticity through the same transported center labels; they are different particles across runs. Their centers are not relocated to a new grid-dependent neck. A separate baseline uses the same reselection recipe as the refinements; its full-window gap differences from the archived endpoint selection are at most 2.58e-7.

A source-inspection correction is necessary for new n=24 passive observers: floating FFT mode labels ±6.999999999999999 must be rounded before integer indexing. The original truncating conversion otherwise duplicates ±6 and omits ±7. The corrected analytic mode-7 check has maximum error 2.61e-15 versus 0.841 for truncation. The original n=16 exact replay retains its original evaluator; no original solver file was edited and no fluid equation was changed. The original runner and sealed sources remain in the local archive. Selected algorithm sources are included here for inspection; their publication path changes are recorded in provenance.json.

The first baseline instrumentation attempt appended observers to the original array, changing three coordinate components by 1.08e-19 through arithmetic shape, despite identical physical/material fields. It was stopped on the strict equality assertion and preserved. The corrected baseline carries added observers separately, leaving the original arithmetic shape intact; it passed all 16 state/801-marker exact comparisons. No tolerance was relaxed.

At 51 common times in [1.1,1.2], curl(u) is traced forward from moving A and its periodic nearest distance to moving B is measured on line segments over 0.375 and 0.75 arclengths. The first grid128/ds1/512 readout failed the 5e-5 tracing criterion at one n32 frame (5.3636e-5 direct discrepancy at t=1.196). That result is retained. Final comparisons therefore use the uniformly refined cubic Fourier grid192 and RK4 ds=1/1024, checked against grid256/ds1/2048 over all 51 times for all six runs. The tolerance, fluid states and particles were not changed. Direct Fourier tracing checks both endpoints and adaptively chosen most-sensitive interior frames. Prime-37 endpoint selection is additionally compared with direct ds=1/2048 tracing. A near-null direction below 1e-8 would stop a trace. The final minimum direction magnitudes and actual check coverage are saved in receipts.

All practical targets were recorded in experiment-plan.md before refinement interpretation: maximum pair gap change ≤0.001, maximum-over-time median absolute pair change ≤0.0005, and decreasing differences; final velocity relative L2 <1%, vorticity <5%, with decreasing differences; tracing gap sensitivity ≤0.00005. These are pragmatic numerical criteria, not mathematical error bounds. Time convergence is evaluated independently at n16 and with the last n32 halving. Space convergence uses n16/n24/n32 at the common dt 0.0005. Common-grid field comparisons use a 64³ physical lattice; velocity/curl retain complete evolved Fourier modes, while C uses its complete finite-volume spectrum with correct Nyquist splitting. Excess-temperature relative errors use theta, not 1+theta, as the denominator. The field-reading grid is not a new simulation.

Gap magnitude and the location attaining it are different observables. Closest-arclength shifts larger than 0.02 and finite-window endpoint minima are reported separately. Nearly tied minima can exchange their selected location while the nearest distance changes little. The nested-window inequality is a property of a minimum over larger sets and does not establish flow accuracy, a unique physical neck or complete vortex-tube identity.

Diagnostics include independent source/context ledgers, total energy minus external and event-write work, spectral divergence, material determinant error and minimum temperature. Their smallness is a check on the discrete computation, not external physical validation. No extra runs were added simply to obtain a favorable result. The full raw fields and observer histories remain in the local archive. This compact publication includes the per-pair measurements, finer tracing controls, summaries, diagnostics, event receipts and selected algorithm sources. It does not contain the full field arrays or support a complete standalone solver replay. SHA256 receipts record source preservation.
