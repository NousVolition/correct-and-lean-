# Actual fluid and time refinement: declared before interpretation

Scope: rerun the archived threshold 0.015, breathing-on, 801-marker Hug v3 coupled system from model time 0 to 1.2. The 10% Q-neighborhood identifies the archived 25-pair observer bundle; Q does not cut the vortex traces. The 0.375 and 0.75 forward arclength windows are retained. This is numerical evidence, not a Lean proof or physical calibration.

1. Reproduce the original 16³, dt 0.002 baseline with the original solver code and event ordering. Compare every available native saved state and all 801 prime-time marker positions, and regenerate the gap measurement.
2. Time refinement: 16³ with dt 0.002, 0.001, 0.0005. Spatial refinement: 16³, 24³, 32³ at dt 0.0005; use 32³ at dt 0.00025 to bound remaining time error. A short runtime measurement may justify a smaller intermediate spatial sequence, but any change must be recorded before results are interpreted.
3. Match initial physical fields exactly: lift the archived 16³ Fourier polynomial for initial heat to each finer grid, while retaining zero initial velocity/context and all identical material, social, and observer initial states. Do not initialize finer grids from an evolved coarse state. Keep the 384 material quadrature points, smoothing widths, physical forcing (peak 0.5, period 0.6), and all physical event start/write times unchanged. The spatial experiment refines the Eulerian fluid discretization only, not material quadrature or model laws. Event payloads are recalculated from each evolving state by the unchanged quantized write law.
4. Observer conventions: (a) fixed material labels, obtained by inverting the original baseline Heun maps from the archived prime-37 A/B/center positions to time zero, then transported independently by each run; (b) per-run line reselection, select ±0.09375 arclength on each run's prime-37 vorticity through the same transported center labels, then transport those new A/B points. Convention (a) preserves particle identity but need not begin with zero gap on each refined run. Convention (b) preserves the selection procedure, but compares different A/B particles across runs. Report both and never conflate them.

Metrics and decision rules fixed before interpreting refinement results:

- Compare 51 common times in [1.1,1.2], with min/median/max of each window's 25 gaps, per-pair errors, window benefit (local minus extended), and change from the initial gap. Report final-window differences, their signs and which observers attain them.
- Primary gap agreement target: last refinement max absolute per-pair difference at most 0.001 cube lengths, and median difference at most 0.0005, with successive differences decreasing. This is a declared practical numerical target, not a theorem. Report exact values even when a target fails. A qualitative sign is robust only if it exceeds the measured time/tracing uncertainty; a nested-window inequality alone does not establish physical invariance.
- Compare common-grid L2 and maximum differences of velocity, vorticity, C and temperature; use relative L2 differences scaled by the finer field's RMS. Practical field target: less than 1% velocity and 5% vorticity relative L2 difference at the final refinement, with decreasing successive differences. C and temperature are reported without a separate acceptance threshold.
- Report divergence, context mass balance, total energy minus external and event-write work, determinant error, and minimum temperature at prime times. Divergence/context balance should remain near roundoff (absolute 1e-10); energy defect should decrease under time refinement. Energy accounting is a diagnostic of the discrete model, not external physical validation.
- Keep postprocessing error separate: trace with RK4 in arclength, compare 1/512 vs 1/1024 and denser cubic Fourier interpolation; compare endpoint gaps against direct Fourier evaluation where affordable. Target gap sensitivity at most 0.00005, and report failures/near-null directions.
- Completion means the baseline and declared refinement levels have actually run or a specific failure has been demonstrated, tables and reproducible sources are saved, and limitations are explicit. A failure to converge is a valid result; a failed or unperformed level is not numerical validation.

Original sources and archived receipts remain unchanged. Results are written only under this study's work/fluid-convergence and outputs/fluid-convergence directories.

Pre-run source inspection addition: at n=24, NumPy returns the FFT label 7 as 6.999999999999999. The archived passive velocity evaluator truncates labels with astype(int), which would duplicate mode 6 and omit 7 on the new grid. The refinement runner rounds these integer-valued mode labels before indexing, leaving all fluid equations untouched. The n=16 baseline retains the original evaluator. This correction is exercised by a direct-Fourier analytic consistency check before the n=24 run.

Closest-point position is a separate observable. Report changes in closest arclength, flag shifts larger than 0.02 cube lengths, and report any local/extended endpoint minimum. A stable distance does not establish stable minimizer identity, and neither establishes identity of complete vortex tubes.

## Measurement refinement triggered by a failed check

After the n32/dt0.0005 integration completed, a direct Fourier check at t=1.196 measured a 5.3636e-5 gap difference from grid128/ds1/512, exceeding the unchanged 5e-5 tracing criterion. The old result and receipt are retained. The final common primary readout is therefore refined uniformly to grid192/ds1/1024 for all six runs, with one finite control level grid256/ds1/2048 over all 51 times for all six runs. The target, fluid runs and particle identities are unchanged. This single recovery comprises those six postprocessing controls; if they fail, report unresolved tracing sensitivity rather than lowering the criterion or continuing an unbounded refinement sequence. Direct endpoint and worst-interior-frame controls remain part of the evidence.
