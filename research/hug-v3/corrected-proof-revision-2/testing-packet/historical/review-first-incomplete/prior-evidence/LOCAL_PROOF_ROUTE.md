# Candidate local proof route for the complete v3 rates

This is an analytical route, not a completed Lean proof. It concerns the real-arithmetic interpretation of the source's fixed finite spatial realization. It does not replace the spatial operators, freeze memory, omit any field or establish an infinite-dimensional continuum theorem.

Fix a valid decoded fabric region, a fixed event schedule and all declared source inputs. Between events, decoded finite records are constant as records while their current relevance and all continuous states evolve. The complete state dictionary is a finite-dimensional real vector. Auxiliary captured event-start deformation arrays are retained unchanged until their specified use.

On an open neighborhood with nonzero material directions and strict decoding margins, the candidate local argument is:

1. Fourier transforms, differentiation, projection, masks, finite-volume difference maps, interpolation sums and fixed component selections are finite linear or finite-sum operations. They do not cause an unbounded spatial derivative operator in a fixed finite-grid norm. This says nothing about bounds uniform in resolution.
2. Products, exponentials, sigmoid and the declared bounded constitutive expressions are locally Lipschitz wherever their denominators are nonzero. Norms and positive/negative parts are locally Lipschitz even where not differentiable. A proof using only a C1 tactic would unnecessarily exclude some source expressions.
3. The source's exact-off activation has a flat join at zero. Its regularity must be established for that actual formula. The positive delta already selected in v3 regularizes the gradient magnitude; it must not be confused with an alteration to the earlier original-norm source branch.
4. The normalized thermal kernel has a positive denominator. Its finite image sum is even, so endpoint values agree across the periodic wrap. Piecewise derivative bounds could give local Lipschitz control across that wrap; global smoothness cannot simply be assumed from its Gaussian pieces.
5. For finite states, positive viscosity makes Qv nonnegative; source weights are nonnegative; the strictly positive power floor controls the writing-allocation denominator. The positive retrieval prior controls its normalization. These inequalities require proofs for the exact operators used.
6. At strict valid decoding margins, the finite sign pattern is locally constant. Therefore decoded records and CRC status are locally fixed while the relevance weights remain functions of the entire current state. The piecewise constant decoder must not be replaced by a smooth invented memory function.
7. Those properties would yield the complete local Lipschitz and time-continuity hypotheses needed by the pinned mathlib Picard-Lindelof and uniqueness theorems. They have not yet been instantiated for the full v3 RHS in Lean.

This route would establish a local segment while it remains inside a valid region. It does not establish that the segment reaches the next scheduled event or the requested final time. A read permitted exactly at its margin need not have an open neighborhood of permitted reads. Continuation requires additional bounds from the actual evolution.

At a scheduled event, the complete update includes record construction from the retained start deformation, quantization, validation, writing work, structural-state replacement and heat. The source's finite input bounds and budget checks are mathematical obligations for successful execution. A deterministic rejection is not a solution to the intended successful-trajectory theorem. If a write succeeds and its output satisfies the next local hypotheses, event-by-event uniqueness can then be assembled without changing the equations.

None of these unproved regularity, continuation or correspondence obligations is added to the Lean solution as an axiom or disguised as an already established property.
