Meaning scale audit
30 October 2026

Finding

In the current A/S candidate, z=1 is the endpoint of a proposed qualification
weight for one idea. It does not mean maximum knowledge, complete understanding,
or the limit of a person's development. The equation and its assumptions were
not changed during this audit.

The same agent and idea are retained: “Giving someone space to speak can change
what I understand.” Smoke and Clay remain that agent's inward speaking and
listening positions. Their separate z values describe the qualification carried
by this same idea at each position.

What zero and one mean

The active protocol explicitly records:
  z=0: “Giving space permits new information to change what I understand.”
  z=1: “Giving space is conditional on confirming my existing interpretation.”
Intermediate values are assumed weights of the imposed qualification. They are
not measured percentages of a person, population fractions, a second idea, or
a total quantity of understanding.

This definition is in work/monoxide-buildup/protocol.json, semantic_coordinate.
Assumption A5 explicitly says the one-dimensional coordinate, imposed endpoint
and distances are analyst choices needing author/person review. The scale was
introduced in the Monoxide buildup candidate; the earlier equation register did
not contain this z dynamics. The existing report also labels the scales provisional.

Above one in the unchanged equations

The meaning differential equation can algebraically be evaluated above one.
For example, setting both meaning states to 1.2 and setting interaction and
clarification rates to zero gives zero meaning drift, so both stay at 1.2.
This deliberately violates StateOK and is outside the invariance theorem.

However, unrestricted z>1 is incompatible with the full candidate's unchanged
registration rule E8:
  Pr(C=1 | B) = eta B (1 - z_Clay v).

For an expressed challenging contribution, B=1 and v=1. Using the existing
illustrative eta=0.8 gives the following exact rational results:

  z_Clay     Challenging contribution     Confirming contribution (v=0)
  0          0.8                         0.8
  0.5        0.4                         0.8
  1          0                           0.8
  1.2       -0.16                        0.8
  2         -0.8                         0.8

A negative result cannot be a probability. For any eta>0, B=1 and v=1, every
z_Clay>1 has this problem. Zero eta/B or a confirming contribution can mask it;
that does not make unrestricted above-one states valid for all allowed encounters.
This is a check of the existing formula, not a revised probability or a clipped
output. No participant observations or new Lean/Comparator run are claimed here.

Decision and missing connection

Retain [0,1] for z under the current provisional qualification-weight meaning.
Allowing above-one values as general valid states of the full candidate would
require a separately justified semantic and mathematical revision. This audit
makes no such revision. Values above one are useful out-of-domain diagnostics.

Increasing accumulated experience, influence or retained adverse exposure does
not require z>1: A and S have no unit-interval restriction, and L has no upper
bound of one. Saturating effects do not imply that the accumulated load stops.
The later understanding update H is a separate construct, not identical to z.

What remains unresolved is whether this proposed qualification axis faithfully
represents the author's intended idea and a person's reported experience. The
missing items are author/person-reviewed endpoint meanings; an observation rule
for assigning intermediate values with uncertainty; evidence that a single axis
is sufficient; and evidence for linking that coordinate to selective registration.
More severe pressure, a new kind of distortion, and more understanding cannot
automatically be encoded as “z greater than 1” without specifying what changes.

The Lean invariance proof remains valid under its original premises. It proves
preservation of the declared interval, not the adequacy of that social scale.
The assistant's earlier framing of this as a possible ceiling on understanding
was too broad; the active protocol assigns z the narrower qualification meaning.
