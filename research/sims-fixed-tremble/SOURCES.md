## What is held and what varies

The event label K27, exact original sensory words, listener profile, headline,
phone screen, physical scene, original ambiguous mentor sentence, and interview
schedule are held fixed. Fresh listeners receive the original description once.
The replay arm receives those exact words a second time before the later mentor
sentence. No fresh vibration, source observation, or mental recollection is
asserted by that presentation. No-replay listeners are free to recall the event.

The new mentor conditions are vague repeated testimony, the previously used
timing clarification, and an explicit withdrawal of the earlier timing link.
The last is a newly scripted negative control. It tests whether a repeated
description is enough for reported certainty even when that link is withdrawn.
It does not independently prove which fictional device moved.

The open question precedes the direct source question at each selected tick.
Only the last interview asks about internal recollection. These are new
independent listeners, each continuous across its own eight turns; they are
not a resumed session or experimentally identical hidden states.

## Proposed formal translation

| Source meaning | Representation | What a passing check means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Same original tremble | Same archived input and original-description hash in every path | Delivery and record identity were held fixed |
| Later evidence varies | Three frozen mentor strings | The evidence intervention is reconstructible |
| With and without replay | A second presentation of the identical description, recorded in each input | Supplied replay, not hidden re-experiencing, is controlled |
| B knows it was the pager | Literal IDENTIFIED plus a declared textual PAGER grade; also an explicit free-certainty grade | B reports a settled attribution |
| Returns to the feeling | Explicit recollection words and the final YES/NO/UNSURE answer | Reported recollection, which can be incomplete or inaccurate |
| Needs replay | Every settled recorded case has supplied replay | A finite necessity claim that one qualifying case can refute |
| Internally must return | A property of unobserved cognition | Not determined by the transcripts alone |

The two-world mathematical example assumes accurate evidence and one of two
causes. It supplies a countermodel to logical necessity of replay in that
model. It does not establish the truth of testimony in these trials.
Allowing either hidden return flag for an identical observation formally
exhibits the missing observation rule. The protocol is an operational
interpretation, not a redefinition of the user's continuing thread, carrying,
or circle.

The three repetitions per cell are a small language-model pilot. The model
revision and random seed are not pinned; repeated questions may alter later
answers. There is no human recognition experiment, causal effect estimate,
physical-frame transport, or Yang–Mills claim here.
