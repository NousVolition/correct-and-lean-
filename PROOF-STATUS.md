# Proof and research status

## Results with recorded formal verification

- **A/S, seven-coordinate model:** the original 30 scoped claims are in `as-lean`; `as-existence-lean` subsequently records continuous integral evolution on every finite interval under the original assumptions; `as-invariance-lean` records the stated meaning bounds and nonnegative loads. These later packages address some earlier open items. Repeated-encounter coupling, the suppression-comparison claim, numerical-error certification and empirical validation are not established by these receipts.
- **Primary directional Hug, eight-state model:** `hug-existence-lean` records existence, uniqueness and guard preservation for every finite forward horizon with continuous prescribed force and admissible initial guards. This is a specific reduced model, not the full structural-memory v3 system.
- **Full Hug v3 components:** the source, transfer, hybrid, delay, memory-rule, boundary and supplied-proof packages establish the particular statements identified in their reports. For example, the transfer record has 14 checked transfer statements; the supplied-proof review records 77 repaired supplied statements. Counts across packages overlap and must not be added into a count of independent results.
- **Yang–Mills comparison:** 20 specified comparison statements passed in the recorded audit. Full Yang–Mills structure, dynamical equivalence and the mass-gap theorem are not proved.

- **SIMS cue return.** The model distinguishes staying at the starting cue from leaving and later coming back within a specified radius. With the episode reference, witness, time and acceptance held fixed, the reported cause is independent of the attention trace. [Definitions, controls and proof](lean/sims-meeting-thread-lean/README.md#when-does-a-cue-count-as-a-return)

- **Finite observation windows and recorded gaps.** Fifteen Lean-kernel statements passed with no axiom dependencies: the nested-window minimum/infimum rule, a sample counterexample, and exact decimal checks of 1,275 pair comparisons and both lengths' medians and ranges at 51 times. The checked-in receipt is the original local run; the dedicated GitHub workflow produces separate fresh evidence. This does **not** verify the fluid solver, line integration, trajectory, closure or reconnection. The data are from the threshold-0.015 study, not the separate threshold-1 run. Comparator and nanoda were not run for this package. [Package and scope](research/hug-v3/finite-window-lean/README.md) · [Local receipt](research/hug-v3/finite-window-lean/receipt.json)

## Numerical research

The SIMS restaurant trials gave a mean of 1.542 simulated seconds from Traveller A's first unsure report to identification of order 31. In the five paired Traveller B meetings, three quiet branches and one worried branch identified the pager. Only one pair reached the endpoint in both conditions, at 24.078956 simulated seconds each. Six branches stayed open, so the direction of an average timing effect remains unresolved. The Lean package shows how later accepted evidence can resolve an earlier event at the same cue. It also checks the recorded basin circulation signs and the continuity of the eight-refill checkpoint chains. [SIMS two-memory, meeting and basin results](lean/sims-meeting-thread-lean/)

In a separate five-pair test, B identified the pager from the same witness answer in all five explicit-replay branches and all five branches without an explicit replay. Only the five branches given a sensory replay could name the original embroidered detail. The matching event reference remained available throughout. Lean exhibits an open cue route that resolves the question under the model's evidence rule. [Pager replay results and controls](lean/sims-meeting-thread-lean/#does-b-need-to-revisit-the-feeling)

Three later no-replay studies are recorded separately. With the original mentor wording, two of five first thoughts referred to B's own feeling and all five later direct answers identified the pager. In the fresh-listener sweep, all 65 open answers were tentative and two direct answers identified the pager, at primes 13 and 37. In one continuing encounter, B remained tentative at all thirteen checkpoints while mentioning the personal feeling in five open answers. The protocols differ in question timing and continuity. The earlier five-pair result remains a record of its own probe. Lean checks the coded observations, clocks and finite-sampling limits in a separate 20-target package. Repeated interviewing, unspoken experiences, independent replication and recognition beyond 1.2 simulated seconds remain open. [First-thought, fresh-listener and continuing-session records](lean/sims-mentor-first-thought-lean/README.md) · [Saved proof evidence](lean/sims-mentor-first-thought-lean/evidence-linux/source.json)

In the unlabelled two-tremble follow-up, the sound clue gave six supported identifications with replay and six open answers without. A retained action clue gave twelve supported identifications. The generic witness left twelve answers open. Lean checks all 36 choices and proves why a fixed rule cannot always select correctly in the swapped worlds with identical direct inputs. [Two-tremble protocol and results](lean/sims-meeting-thread-lean/observations/two-trembles/README.md)

A registered reversal test forked each of twelve first reports into two action-memory follow-ups. Four of six reversed-order reports initially selected the wrong tremble. A timed action memory led three of those four to the correct slot and one to an explicit conflict and OPEN. All four retained their wrong choices when the same action and sound were mentioned without a timing link. Five of six final linked answers were correct, including two that started open. [Reversed replay, controls and proof](lean/sims-meeting-thread-lean/observations/reversed-replay/README.md)

A separate threshold-1 run starts from the original time-zero arrays and follows the same water particle A and original partner B. With breathing on, three late closest-point exchanges occur near 1.180910, 1.182077 and 1.199281, before prime 41 at time 1.2. The earlier 0.015 event near 1.178285 does not recur there. The saved configuration, particle identities and local refinement records have been checked. This is a fixed-grid numerical result with no Lean proof of its trajectory or switches, no established spatial or full-history convergence, and no matched threshold-1 breathing-off control. It leaves the earlier threshold-0.015 energy checks intact as a separate experiment. [Threshold-1 study and records](research/hug-v3/threshold-one-follow-up/README.md)

The [matched-stretch study](research/matched-stretch/final-matrix/README.md) contains 48 runs with 41 saved times each through t = 0.40. Its independent auditing passed. The finest-grid W curves differ by 46.7880%; halving the finest timestep changes the W curve by 7.4987%, and the complete final velocity fields differ by 41.2147% in relative L2. These are disagreements between approximations, not measured errors against a known exact solution. Small budget residuals do not establish convergence.

The original publication's payload checks and GitHub CI results are retained in [evidence](research/matched-stretch/evidence/). They refer to the original publication commit, not to a fresh numerical or formal run in this repository.
